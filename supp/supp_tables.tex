\begin{table}[h!]

\caption{\textbf{Theories of drift under selection compute the same quantity, an ancestor's expected long-term genetic contribution $R$, from its heritable selective advantage.} $R$ is the ancestor's share of the population's genes many generations later, relative to an average ancestor's. At linked sites, the long-term contribution that sets drift is that of the chromosomal segment carrying the focal neutral copy, not that of the individual ancestor.}
\label{tab:models}
\small
\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.5cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.6cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.0cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.2cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.0cm}}
\toprule
Theory & Expected long-term contribution ($R$) & Persistence of the advantage & Regime of validity & Role in this study \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{5}{l}{\textit{Unlinked sites: the ancestor's contribution}} \\
Robertson~\cite{robertson1961}; Santiago and Caballero~\cite{santiago1995} & Linear in the ancestor's breeding value, with slope $Q$ & Constant retention factor $Z$ per generation, treated as a parameter & Small $V_A$; any smooth mapping from breeding value to fitness & Defines $Q$, the small-variance approximation, and $Z_k$ at MSDB \\
\addlinespace
Woolliams et al.~\cite{woolliams1999} & Linear in several advantages at once (focal individual and social partner)& Matrix recursion across sexes and partners at retention factor $Z$ & Small $V_A$ & Derives $\kappa(\alpha)$ and partitions its focal, partner, and \textit{cis} components \\
\addlinespace
Barton and Etheridge~\cite{barton2011}; Weissman and Barton~\cite{weissman2012} & Exponential in a Gaussian breeding value for log-fitness ($e^{Qz}$) & $Z = 1$ exactly & Any $\mathrm{Var}(z)$ under $w \propto e^{z}$ (given unbounded tail) & Gives the multiplicative model, the multiplicative bound, and $\kappa_\text{max}$\\
\midrule
\multicolumn{5}{l}{\textit{Linked sites: the chromosomal segment's contribution}} \\
Santiago and Caballero~\cite{santiago1998}; Buffalo and Kern~\cite{buffaloKern2024} & Linear in the segment's \textit{cis} breeding value, with slope $1/(1-\rho)$ & Joint decay via recombination and purging, $\rho = (1-r)Z$ per generation & Small segment variance; $Z_k$ set by MSDB & Parameterizes the reduction at linked sites and $\overline{\Omega}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
